Why cement bricks?

Cement bricks are stronger and more dimensionally consistent than clay bricks, save mortar and labour time, and are approved on public and private projects.

Available sizes

Solid 25×12×6 cm for load-bearing walls, hollow 25×12×13 cm, concrete blocks 20×20×40 cm in 10, 15 and 20 cm thickness, and interlock pavers for walkways.

Quality & testing

Compressive strength and absorption test results from accredited labs are available on request for consultant approval.

FAQ

What is the price of 1,000 cement bricks?

It depends on size, quantity and delivery point. Send size and quantity on WhatsApp for a delivered price.

Cement bricks or red clay bricks?

Cement bricks are stronger, consistent in size and save mortar; they are now the standard on most projects.

Do you provide test certificates?

Yes, compressive strength and absorption results from an accredited lab are available on request.
