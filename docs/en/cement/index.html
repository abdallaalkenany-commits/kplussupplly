Cement types we supply

Ordinary Portland Cement (CEM I 42.5N) for reinforced concrete, blended cement (CEM II 32.5) for masonry and plastering, sulphate-resisting cement (SRC) for foundations and saline soils, and white cement for finishing works. Every shipment complies with Egyptian Standard ES 4756.

How is the cement price set?

Price per ton depends on type, plant, quantity and delivery location. Send us the quantity, type and site location and we will reply with an all-in delivered quote within hours, with discounts for large volumes and monthly contractor agreements.

Delivery to your site

Our fleet covers Greater Cairo, the New Administrative Capital, Ain Sokhna, Upper Egypt, the Delta and the North Coast — 50 kg bags on trailers, or bulk for ready-mix plants.

FAQ

What is the cement price per ton today?

Prices change daily by plant, type, quantity and delivery point. Message us on WhatsApp for today's delivered price.

What is the difference between OPC and SRC?

SRC has lower tricalcium aluminate, so it resists sulphate attack from soil and saline water and is used for foundations and coastal structures. OPC suits general structural works.

What is the minimum order?

From 10 tons (one truck) upward, with monthly supply contracts available for contractors.

Do you deliver to Upper Egypt and the North Coast?

Yes, we deliver to all governorates; freight is priced by distance and quantity.
