Why fire doors are mandatory

Egypt's civil defence code requires fire-rated doors in stairwells, emergency exits and electrical rooms of towers, malls, hospitals and schools.

Door components

Fire-resistant core, insulated frame, optional fire glass, intumescent seal, hydraulic closer and tested hinges and handles.
